# Hi there, I'm Daniel Cahen 👋

I am a **PhD Botanist at the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew**, specializing in the family **Rhamnaceae**. 

## 🛠 The Biologist's Toolkit
I am currently developing a suite of experimental AI-powered prototypes designed to assist with taxonomic workflows and identification.

- 🌿 **[Flora Scout](https://github.com/gouania/flora-scout)**: A search-grounded multi-access key for indeterminate specimens.
- 🧬 **[Taxon Profiles](https://github.com/gouania/taxon-profiles)**: Diagnostic descriptions and side-by-side species comparisons.
- 📋 **[BioKey AI](https://github.com/gouania/biokey-ai)**: Generating identification keys from raw text descriptions.
- 🌲 **[Cedrus-BioKey Pilot](https://github.com/gouania/cedrus-biokey-pilot)**: A "Strict Mode" identification interface using fixed datasets.
- 📑 **[Scientific Name Extractor](https://github.com/gouania/scientific-name-extractor)**: High-speed utility for cleaning and extracting taxonomic names.
- 📜 **[Botanical Authorities](https://github.com/gouania/botanical-authorities)**: Biographical search engine for plant taxonomists.

## 🔬 Research Interests
- Seed banking; identifying and curating Millennium Seed Bank Partnership herbarium collections.
- Systematics of Rhamnaceae (*Gouania*, *Smythea*, *Ventilago*, *Ziziphus*)
- European Flora & Field Identification
- AI Reasoning in Systematic Botany
